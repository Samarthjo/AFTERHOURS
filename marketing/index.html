NIGHT DESK / FAQ

Before you clock in.

Do I need to install anything?+

No. AFTERHOURS runs in a modern mobile or desktop browser.

Can I play alone?+

You can join at any time, but uneven roles and shared problems make the city better when other people answer the signal.

What happens when I leave?+

The city keeps ticking. When you return, you get a short report of what changed while you were gone.

Is this a match?+

Not exactly. It is a persistent cooperative city. A civilization lasts until it collapses—or its citizens begin again.

What data do you collect?+

We use anonymous campaign events and aggregate game outcomes to improve onboarding and balance. Signup details are held separately from the landing event stream.
